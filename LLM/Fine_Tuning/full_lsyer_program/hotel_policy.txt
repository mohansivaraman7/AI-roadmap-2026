Q: What time is check-in at Seaview Hotel?
A: Check-in starts at 2 PM.

Q: What time is check-out?
A: Check-out is at 11 AM.

Q: Can I get a late check-out?
A: Late check-out until 2 PM is available for a fee of 30 dollars.

Q: Are pets allowed?
A: Dogs under 10 kg are allowed for 25 dollars per night.

Q: When is breakfast served?
A: Breakfast is served from 7 AM to 10:30 AM.

Q: What is the cancellation policy?
A: Cancellation is free up to 48 hours before arrival.

Q: Is Wi-Fi free?
A: Wi-Fi is free in all rooms and public areas.

Q: How much does parking cost?
A: Parking costs 12 dollars per day.
